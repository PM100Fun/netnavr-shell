# Code of Conduct

NetNavr contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and
constructive.

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information
  about another person.
- Respect security disclosure boundaries and do not pressure reporters to
  publish exploit details.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior is harmful to the community. Report conduct concerns privately to
the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as
confidentially and promptly as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
